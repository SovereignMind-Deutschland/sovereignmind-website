Local or cloud? A factual comparison

This page compares a locally operated AI assistant (SovereignMind) with three types of providers: cloud assistants with a license per user, a self-built cloud chat, and freely available interfaces for self-hosting. Figures as of: October 2026.

Important: The cost figures are a worked example with open assumptions, not a price list and not an offer. SovereignMind is currently provided only as a demo; no license price has been set, which is why the SovereignMind rows are calculated without a license fee. The manufacturers’ current information always prevails. Third-party prices partly come from third-party overviews and must be checked before a decision (see sources).

In short: Local fits confidential documents and many users; cloud fits the strongest models, few users and little wish to operate anything. The term is explained in What is an on-premises AI assistant?

What fits when?

Local fits if …

documents must not leave the building (professional secrecy, personnel, patient or design data), many users share the same installation and fixed rather than usage-based costs are wanted.

Cloud fits if …

the strongest available language models are needed, you want no operation of your own and no hardware, or the number of users fluctuates strongly. With few users a license per user is usually cheaper (see cost example).

Combining both

Many organizations separate: highly sensitive documents run locally, less sensitive tasks with high demands on the model in a controlled cloud access. The data protection officer decides, not this page.

Cost example

Net in euros, including all one-off costs (hardware, setup), for the first year and over 36 months. Assumptions: 20 questions per user and working day, 220 working days a year, about 6,000 input and 500 output tokens per question, hourly rate €90, 1 US dollar = €0.90. Hardware and electricity for SovereignMind are estimates (one workstation with a graphics card, several devices at 200 users), not measured values. Working time is your employees’ internal time (setup, operation), valued at the hourly rate, and is shown separately for each row; for cloud licenses, 4 hours of setup and 1 hour per month (users, permissions, reviews) are assumed. Self-operated counts setup and operating hours of your own IT; with a service provider a range for setup and a maintenance contract.

How to read this: With few users a cloud license is cheaper; in the first year SovereignMind incurs hardware and setup in full, which is why local is cheaper in this example only from about 100 users; over 36 months, during which the hardware is used, already from about 27 to 36 users (in each case without a license fee for SovereignMind). Model quality, support and the effort of data protection reviews are not converted into money. The office-suite rows lack the required base license.
