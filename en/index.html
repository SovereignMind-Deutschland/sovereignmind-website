Why local?

  1. Your data stays in-house.

    No connection to cloud AI.

  2. Every answer names its source.

    Clickable in the original document.

  3. Runs as a background service.

    Without Docker, with a tray app or a command.

What would you use it for? · Local or cloud?

From file to answer

  1. Add a document

    PDFs, DOCX files and spreadsheets are read.

  2. Split

    The text is split into short, searchable sections.

  3. Search

    The matching passages are found for the question.

  4. Answer with source

    The model answers in the language of the question, citation included.
