< <strong>Signature of the checksums (minisign):</strong> Checksums detect damaged downloads, but not a swapped release: whoever swaps the release can also swap <code>SHA256SUMS.txt</code>. That is why the signature <code>SHA256SUMS.txt.minisig</code> sits next to it. It is created offline with our private key; the one-liner scripts and this page contain the public key, so it comes from a different place than the release.

< <strong>How to read this:</strong> With few users a cloud license is cheaper; in the first year SovereignMind incurs hardware and setup in full, which is why local is cheaper in this example only from about 100 users; over 36 months, during which the hardware is used, already from about 27 to 36 users (in each case without a license fee for SovereignMind). Model quality, support and the effort of data protection reviews are not converted into money. The office-suite rows lack the required base license.
